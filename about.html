About us

The first Shamrock Shuffle happened because a national bar-crawl company came through Milwaukee, took everyone's money, ran a bad day out, and left. We thought we could do a better one for the same street, so we did.

Eighteen Shuffles later there are three events a year, sixty bars who take our calls, and a lot of people who have been to all of them. We still do not have an office. We do have a spreadsheet, and it is a good spreadsheet.

The whole thing runs on the same idea it started with: the people organising the crawl should be people who would go on the crawl.
