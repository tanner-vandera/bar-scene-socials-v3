01 — Who we are

More than
a bar crawl

We started this because the bar crawls we went to were run by companies three states away who had never set foot on Brady Street. Ours are put together by people who drink here.

Three days a year, a few thousand of us take over a street, wear something stupid, and hand a chunk of the money to a Milwaukee charity. That is the entire business plan and it has worked for eight years.
