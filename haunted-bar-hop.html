Brady Street.
Sixteen bars.
One stupid afternoon.

Every other Halloween crawl in this city runs on Water Street or 3rd, which is fine if you enjoy queueing behind four hundred people in the same nurse costume. We do ours on Brady, because Brady is where the bars have character and the bartenders know your name by the third stop.

Doors at three in the afternoon. It is a long, silly, well-organised day, and it ends at an afterparty you did not plan for.

yes, all of it. no tiers, no upsell.
